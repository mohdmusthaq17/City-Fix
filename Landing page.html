How NagrikX works

From a photo on your phone to a confirmed fix.

  1. Report

    Upload a photo. Not sure of the category? AI suggests one.

  2. Pin the spot

    Tap the map or use your location, so the city knows exactly where.

  3. Track it

    An admin sets it to Pending, In Progress or Resolved. You see every change.

  4. Confirm

    Say yes if it is fixed. Say no and it goes back to the admin.

What you get

For citizens and for the admins who handle the complaints.

AI category detection

Google Gemini suggests Road Damage, Garbage, Streetlights or Water Leakage. If it is unsure, you choose.

Map pin and GPS

Built on OpenStreetMap. Every complaint keeps its exact coordinates.

Citizen confirmation

A complaint is only closed for good when the person who reported it agrees.

Points and leaderboard

Admins verify genuine reports. Citizens earn 10 points, and the top 10 are shown by first name.

Admin dashboard

Search, filter by status or category, and find disputed complaints quickly.

Safe by design

Hashed passwords, protected forms, image-only uploads, and an AI key kept on the server.
